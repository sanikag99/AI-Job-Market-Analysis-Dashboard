# 🤖 AI Job Market Dashboard

An interactive Tableau dashboard designed to analyze AI job market trends using job listing data. The dashboard provides insights into job demand, salary levels, experience requirements, employment types, geographic hiring patterns, and education requirements across industries.

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The **AI Job Market Dashboard** provides a high-level view of the AI employment landscape through interactive visualizations and KPIs.

### Key Metrics

- **Total Job Openings:** 15,000
- **Total Salary:** $1.73B
- **Average Experience Required:** 6.3 years

## 🔍 Key Analysis

The dashboard includes the following analyses:

### 🌍 Global AI Hiring
Visualizes AI job demand across different countries and regions to identify geographic hiring patterns.

### 💰 Salary by Employment Type
Compares salary distributions across different employment types and highlights variations in compensation.

### 📈 Experience & Job Demand
Shows the relationship between experience levels and job demand across different job categories.

### 💼 Salary vs Experience
Analyzes the relationship between required experience and salary levels.

### 🎓 Education Requirements by Industry
Compares education requirements across industries such as:

- Technology
- Automotive
- Consulting
- Media
- Retail
